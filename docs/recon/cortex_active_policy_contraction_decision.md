## Decision

The retained active-policy spine is smaller than the composed policy that ran
in v2:

- retain prospective task-set formation through UserPromptSubmit;
- retain Stop closure/continuation as the late truthful-closure gate;
- retain `TaskStandardSpine` and SRE tool evidence as shared state/evidence law;
- do not treat PostToolUse task-standard context as earned active policy until
  a redesigned evaluator case proves value over the simple hook.

The next train is `cortex-posttooluse-proof-surface-role-demotion`. It should
not delete product code. It should role-demote/archive PostToolUse-specific
proof surfaces where the general evaluator now preserves the evidence, and it
should leave product behavior untouched.
