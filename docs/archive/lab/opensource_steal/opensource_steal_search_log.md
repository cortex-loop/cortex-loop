Surface: lab

Executive Benefit: widen the theft search beyond known agent repos while keeping the active packet limited to mechanism-level wins that strengthen Cortex as a rich multi-host executive layer.
Why this beats direct product work now: the remaining AUX and support-memory denominator is implementation-heavy, so the fastest safe lift is to mine already-hardened public mechanisms instead of inventing fresh scaffolding.

Governing principle: search by primitive, not by repo hype, and keep license truth separate from mechanism truth.
Executive skill: support-memory substrate, continuity compaction, ingress hygiene, capability law, witness bridges, and bounded codebase orientation.
Product metric: active packet quality rises only when a public mechanism beats the current Hermes / PurpleLlama packet on a real Cortex seam.
Guardrail: no shell-product drift, no Core expansion, no host flattening, and no license-risk code entering the copyable packet.
Kill rule: cut any candidate whose genius is only conceptual, cannot be pinned to exact code and rationale, or does not survive direct comparison against Cortex and the active packet.

## Verdict Legend

- `copy`: materially better implementation of a seam Cortex should already have
- `copy and improve`: real mechanism, but Cortex must tighten the law or types
- `cut`: not better than Cortex or the current packet, or wrong layer

License classes:

- `copyable`: clear permissive license or permissive subtree
- `research-only`: useful idea, but non-permissive or restricted license means `DO NOT COPY`
- `reject`: unclear provenance, unclear license, or not worth keeping

## Surgical Inspection Notes

### mnemo-cortex

What Cortex already has:

- typed `SupportReference` and support-publication carriers
- no persisted ancestry graph from compacted support back to leaf evidence

What mnemo-cortex does better:

- stores explicit `summary_messages` and `summary_sources` edges
- rewrites the active context frontier without discarding ancestry
- exposes `expand_summary(...)` so recall can traverse the summary DAG back to summaries and leaf messages

Why it is active:

- this is the first public mechanism inspected that cleanly maps to Cortex's missing support-lineage seam
- it does not replace Hermes; it complements Hermes by making compacted support explainable

Rationale evidence:

- [tests/test_smoke.py](https://github.com/GuyMannDude/mnemo-cortex/blob/84f2d1af687754e4ef099ceb177c06522583d184/tests/test_smoke.py)
- [CHANGELOG `v2.0.0`](https://github.com/GuyMannDude/mnemo-cortex/blob/84f2d1af687754e4ef099ceb177c06522583d184/CHANGELOG.md)

### sinewave agent-security-scanner-mcp

What Cortex already has:

- no active host-ingress scan seam yet
- PurpleLlama already sets the current bar for preprocess-then-score hostile-context screening

What sinewave does better:

- adds smart de-obfuscation slivers: fenced-code expansion, string defragmentation, NFKD stripping, base64 re-scan, and some multi-turn escalation

Why it is cut:

- the useful parts are buried inside a large heuristic policy engine rather than a clean bounded Cortex mechanism
- too much of the behavior is risk-score accretion and ruleset sprawl, which is the wrong abstraction level for Cortex
- the right move is to remember the de-obfuscation tricks when implementing the PurpleLlama seam, not to promote this repo into the active packet

Rationale evidence:

- [prompt-scanner.test.js](https://github.com/sinewaveai/agent-security-scanner-mcp/blob/0897a85bb06359bcde1f8c1e99c5f345459e8802/scanner-lite/tests/prompt-scanner.test.js)

### `mcp_agent_mail`

License banner:

- `research-only`
- restricted MIT rider
- `DO NOT COPY`

Why it was worth reading:

- it hardens SQLite for high-contention multi-agent workloads with retry, jitter, circuit-breaking, and explicit immediate sessions

Minimal mechanism extract:

```python
await conn.exec_driver_sql("BEGIN IMMEDIATE")
cursor.execute("PRAGMA wal_checkpoint(PASSIVE)")
```

Adaptation note:

- if Cortex later needs heavier SQLite contention hardening around the AUX journal, reimplement the immediate-session and non-blocking checkpoint ideas inside the journal seam rather than importing the SQLAlchemy-wide engine design
