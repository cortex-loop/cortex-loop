# CORTEX Product Charter

Surface: product

Cortex exists to be the executive layer that makes strong models better at larger executive function.

That means the shipped Cortex product should improve:
- continuity across longer work
- bounded control over revision and repair
- verification-aware correction after reality pushes back
- preservation of good structure while fixing falsified structure

The product is not:
- the benchmark harness
- the train loop
- the grader stack
- the active workstream ledger
- the governance apparatus used to prove or manage Cortex

Those supporting systems matter because they help build Cortex, but they are not Cortex the product.

Every future seam in this repository should be judged against the same question:

**Does this make the shipped Cortex executive layer better, or directly unblock proving and building that layer?**
