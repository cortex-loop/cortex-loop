## 1. Governing objective

At the completion boundary, Cortex is trying to do one thing:

**make completion honest.**

The executive expansion is trying to do a different thing:

**make the agent better at hierarchical control, uncertainty handling, branching, reuse, and bounded recovery while it approaches truthful closure.**

The executive expansion is **not** trying to:

- replace the kernel with a soft controller,
- turn confidence, familiarity, elapsed time, or convenience into truth,
- replace canonical deficits with embeddings,
- let geometry certify completion,
- let budgeting authorize completion,
- let humane preferences mutate canonical claims,
- let offline learning rewrite kernel law,
- let adapters author host-native truth.

Biology is imported for better control, not for softer truth.

## 5. The soft-control boundary

The executive layer is authorized to influence only the abstract soft-control family

\[
\mathcal A^{\mathrm{soft}}=
\{\text{stay-course},\text{retrieve},\text{reorient},\text{review},\text{defer},\text{branch},\text{escalate},\text{halt-support}\}.
\]

It may also output auxiliary advisory parameters such as repair ranking and retrieval depth.

`stay-course` is the lawful neutral support action:
- recommend no additional executive intervention beyond quiescent monitoring,
- preserve the current kernel path,
- never certify completion,
- never stand in for missing kernel evidence.

Non-intervention must therefore be expressible positively.
It may not appear only as exhausted search, blocked admissibility, or latched containment.

Soft-control dynamics may use:

- uncertainty,
- typed geometry,
- explicit one-step control scoring,
- memory-conditioned control scoring,
- selective gating,
- host affordances,
- branch caps,
- offline executive memory.

Soft-control dynamics may **not**:

- authorize completion,
- erase canonical deficits,
- mutate canonical claims,
- bypass requirements or invariants.

### 5.1 Catastrophic halt law

Catastrophic safety or integrity halts belong to `H_t` and the kernel verdict path.
They are **not** executive state.

The executive brake `J_t^{\mathrm{brake}}` is only a soft-control latch for cases such as:

- search or branch exhaustion,
- repeated futile reorientation,
- advisory containment before review or escalation,
- controlled stopping of soft exploration.

`halt-support` therefore means:

- not a safety halt,
- not a completion halt,
- not a kernel verdict,
- only a soft recommendation to stop further executive exploration and move to review, escalation, or handoff.

It is distinct from `stay-course`, which means no extra support is needed right now.

This rejects both bad designs:

- **wait out the gate**,
- **hide a second hard halt inside executive state**.
